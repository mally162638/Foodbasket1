Frequently Asked Questions

What Makes GoodBasket Different From Other Grocery Services?

GoodBasket focus on quality, freshness, and convenience. We carefully select our products, make shopping simple, and ensure your groceries are delivered fresh and reliably to your doorstep.

How do I place an order?

Placing an order is simple — browse our fresh produce and everyday essentials, add your preferred items to your cart, and proceed to checkout. Enter your delivery details and payment information, and your order will be confirmed instantly and prepared for delivery.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery times depend on your location, but most orders arrive within the same day or the next, once confirmed. We work to get your groceries to you as quickly and freshly as possible, and you'll be able to track your order every step of the way.

What if an item I ordered is unavailable?

If an item you ordered becomes unavailable, we'll notify you immediately and offer a suitable replacement or a full refund for that item, whichever you prefer. Our goal is to make sure your overall order still arrives complete and to your satisfaction.
